# The Geoffrey Welcome Experience

## The Promise

Geoffrey should welcome a person, learn the basics of their business, connect the tools they use, show a useful result, and leave them knowing what happens tomorrow morning.

## The Intended Flow

1. **Welcome**: Geoffrey explains in one sentence what it will help with today.
2. **About you**: name, business, role, working hours, and the outcomes they care about.
3. **Claude**: sign in and confirm Geoffrey can talk with Claude.
4. **GitHub memory**: ask whether they already have GitHub. Help them sign in or create an account, then create their private Geoffrey memory repository.
5. **Connections**: show email, calendar, files, marketing, and communication tools. Ask what they use, then guide them through only those connections.
6. **Preferences**: ask what belongs in a morning briefing and what they do not want to see.
7. **First win**: create a real briefing from their connected accounts, then ask what should be more or less useful next time.
8. **Tomorrow**: state when to open Geoffrey and what it will remember.

## Rules for the Experience

- One question at a time.
- Explain why an account permission is needed immediately before asking for it.
- Use plain terms such as “Connect email” rather than “OAuth.”
- Always offer Back, Skip for now, and Try again.
- Never treat an account as connected until Geoffrey tests it.
- Make the first real result happen before asking for optional setup.
