# First Geoffrey Demo

After setup, Geoffrey should prove its value immediately.

Do not end with "you are configured." End with one useful action.

## Default: Inbox Rescue

Paste this after Geoffrey is connected:

```text
Geoffrey, run Inbox Rescue.

Use every connected mailbox you can see. Look at the last 7 days. Find important
or unanswered client, prospect, billing, or scheduling messages. Group them by
urgency, draft replies for the top 3, and suggest a realistic plan for handling
the rest today. Do not send anything.
```

## Other First Wins

### Today's Schedule

```text
Geoffrey, build today's schedule.

Review my connected calendars and recent scheduling emails. Build a realistic
plan for today, including focus blocks, meetings, and follow-ups. Do not create
or move events without approval.
```

### Client Follow-Up Finder

```text
Geoffrey, find client follow-ups.

Search recent mail for proposals, open client questions, stalled deals, and
people waiting on a reply. Create a follow-up list and draft messages. Do not
send anything.
```

### Open Loops List

```text
Geoffrey, make my open loops list.

Turn recent email, notes, and projects into a clean list of commitments, waiting
items, and next actions.
```
