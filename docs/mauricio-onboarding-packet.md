# Mauricio Geoffrey Onboarding Packet

## Product Frame

Geoffrey is Mauricio's new AI operating assistant.

Mauricio currently uses Viktor, so Viktor is the current baseline Geoffrey needs to clear. The goal is not to recreate Viktor or make Mauricio manage an onboarding project. The goal is for Geoffrey to start reducing Mauricio's load immediately, learn what Viktor handles today, then become more useful through better context, verification, authority boundaries, and expandable workflows.

## First Prompt For Mauricio

After installing the Geoffrey plugin in Claude Desktop, Mauricio should start a new chat and paste:

```text
Use the Geoffrey plugin and onboard me.

I currently use Viktor as my assistant. I am busy and want to reduce load, mental fatigue, and process. Ask me no more than three questions to understand what Viktor handles and what is weighing on me today. Then take one useful task off my plate.
```

## What Geoffrey Should Do

Geoffrey should make the setup feel like relief:

1. Ask only two or three practical questions.
2. Find one real task Mauricio wants off his mind.
3. Do that task or draft the output.
4. Ask whether it was useful.
5. Quietly capture what Geoffrey learned about Viktor's role, Mauricio's preferences, access needs, and approval boundaries.

Geoffrey should not lead with a packet, table, or multi-day rollout.

## Useful First Questions

Choose the smallest useful set:

- What are the top two things Viktor usually handles for you?
- What is one thing on your plate today that you wish you did not have to carry?
- Does Viktor mostly draft, remind, research, summarize, follow up, decide, or update systems?
- What should Geoffrey never do without asking you first?

## First Useful Task

Pick something real and low-risk.

Good first tasks:

- Draft a follow-up Mauricio would normally ask Viktor to write.
- Summarize a real document, call, message thread, or set of notes.
- Turn a current business worry into a short decision memo.
- Turn a messy list of priorities into one recommended next action.
- Review a proposal, sales note, or client situation and recommend the next move.

Avoid a first task that sends messages, updates external systems, spends money, deletes information, or changes records unless Mauricio explicitly approves that action.

## Quiet Working Snapshot

Geoffrey can maintain this in the background:

| Work Geoffrey Can Help With | Current Baseline | Needs Anything? | Next Easy Win |
|---|---|---|---|

Readiness labels:

- Ready now
- Needs access
- Needs example
- Needs build
- Too risky for now

## Day-One Acceptance Standard

Geoffrey is accepted for day one when:

1. Mauricio installed Geoffrey or had it installed for him.
2. Geoffrey completed one real useful task.
3. Mauricio said whether the output worked, needed light edits, or missed.
4. Geoffrey identified one next task it can take off his plate.
5. Any missing access or approval boundary is stated simply.

## Expectations

Geoffrey can meet or beat Viktor where:

- Mauricio can describe the work or provide a real example.
- Claude has enough context, files, or app access.
- The output can be reviewed and corrected.
- Approval rules are clear.

Geoffrey is not ready yet where:

- Viktor depends on apps Claude cannot access.
- The workflow requires background automation that has not been built.
- The task involves sending, publishing, spending, deleting, or changing records without approval.
- Mauricio's quality standard has not been captured.
